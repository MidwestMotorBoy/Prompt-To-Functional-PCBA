# Notes

## Changed
- design/draw.py, sheet layout: sections used to be packed into rows against a fixed 594 mm page width, giving
  tall narrow pages (USB ports) and sparse, uneven ones. Packing now tries row widths from 250 to 900 mm and picks the one
  whose page is closest to landscape A-series with the least area. Pages are now balanced and less tall.
- Netlist unchanged; make.sh ends with NETLIST OK.
